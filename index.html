V1 · Five lab guides

Use the guides while you practice

1. Watch and try

Pause after an action and repeat it in your own workspace. The guides use Kokoro narration and include captions.

2. Build and measure

Follow Design & Build → Measure → Submit. Verify your connections and DC bias before collecting waveform evidence.

3. Save each lab

Keep your editable project and progress backup. Submit CS and CE separately, with your own circuit, breadboard, and waveform evidence.
